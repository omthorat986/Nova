// 02 ZERO THIRD-PARTY AD TRACKING

NOVA does not sell observer information, does not employ invasive advertising trackers, and does not profile users for commercial marketing. All session tokens use HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax safeguards.

// 03 RIGHT TO DELETION & EXPORT

You hold total sovereignty over your telemetry. You may permanently delete your account at any time via your Observer Profile console. Account deletion immediately cascades across all posts, comments, follows, likes, outreach drafts, and purges S3 assets from storage.
